01 Skill upgrade path

Only levels 12, 16 and 20 matter, since that's where spec slots open. Skills reach 14 naturally, ring lines push the core three to 16, and Arcana does the rest.

  1. Dark Crush → 16Spec 5 removes its cooldown. The class doesn't work until you have it.
  2. Onslaught → 16Your LMB basic. Spec 4 cuts Spinning Strike's cooldown, spec 5 adds Storm Chain.
  3. Spinning Strike → 16Opens the Dark Crush window. Spec 5 ignores Block/Evasion and always crits.
  4. Incandescent Blow → 1616 is enough for this one.
  5. Utility skills → 12Impactful Crush, Wave Blow, Rushing Smash, Recuperation. Tremor Crush to 8.
  6. Defiance → 16Survival during stuns.
  7. Dark Crush → Onslaught → Spinning Strike → 20In that order, through Arcana rolls.

05 Daevanion

  1. Blue skill nodes first: +1 Dark Crush, Onslaught, Spinning Strike and Incandescent Blow on each of the four PvE boards (+4 each).
  2. Orange nodes: Nezekan Combat Speed + CDR, Zikel Damage Boost, Vaizel Crit Dmg Boost, Triniel Multi-Hit Chance.
  3. Green passive nodes: Attack Preparation, Inspiring Spell, Protection Circle, Wind's Promise, Impact Hit, Earth's Promise.
  4. Last: Impactful Crush, Wave Blow, Defiance.
Skip: Max MP, Heat Wave Blow and Gust Rampage nodes, Crossguard, Raging Spell, Multi-Hit Resist, Crit Dmg Tolerance. You can't fill all four boards (570 crystals needed, about 416 obtainable), so skipping these matters. Global has no Ariel board, and Azphel is PvP-only.

06 Macro setup

  1. Build the RMB hotbar line as shown on the right. The bottom slot (Row 0) fires first: Dark Crush, then Spinning Strike, Impactful Crush, Incandescent Blow.
  2. Put Marchutan's Wrath on its own key. You press it by hand to extend the Dark Crush window.
  3. Make the in-game macro: 2 steps, 10 ms each. Step 1 = the Dark Crush line, step 2 = Onslaught.
  4. Turn Skill Queue OFF so the animation cancel works.
  5. Play it: hold LMB (Onslaught) and tap RMB. Cancelling Dark Crush out of Onslaught does 9–12% more damage than holding Dark Crush.
  6. Dark Crush not at 16 yet? Put one opener on its own key and press it whenever Dark Crush comes back.
Never put a mantra in a macro. Mantras are toggles, so the macro switches them off. Keep Undefeated Mantra and Sprint Mantra on their own keys.

07 Rotation and opener

Dark Crush can only be cast for 2 s after Spinning Strike or Impactful Crush, and for 3 s after Marchutan's Wrath. Inside that window you spam it.

  1. Before the pull: Undefeated Mantra and Sprint Mantra on.
  2. Pull: Power of the Storm, then Rushing Smash in.
  3. Open the window: Spinning Strike, then hold the macro (LMB Onslaught + RMB line) to spam Dark Crush.
  4. Burst under Storm: Spin → Dark Crush → Impactful → Dark Crush → Spin → Dark Crush. As the window ends, press Marchutan's Wrath for 3 more seconds.
  5. Upkeep: Power of the Storm right before each boss stagger (call it for the party). Wave Blow in animation gaps. Defiance when stunned.

11 Party role

Your buffs

  • Undefeated Mantra, 100% uptime
  • Power of the Storm before each boss stagger
  • Wave Blow spec 4: boss −15% PvE Damage Boost
  • Earth's Promise and the Protection Circle barrier

These don't stack with a Cleric's: Undefeated vs Light of Protection, Storm vs Earth's Blessing, Earth's Promise vs Chain of Torment, Wave Blow vs Debilitating Mark. Two Chanters only get one mantra.

When to heal

  • Use Recuperation (2 charges) when the party's HP drops.
  • The rest of the time, keep dealing damage. More damage and buffs help the party more than extra healing.
  • If you're the only healer, raise Recuperation to 16 with spec 5 (−3 s CD).

12 5 common mistakes

  1. Putting mantras in a macro. It toggles them off.
  2. Leveling anything before Dark Crush 16. Without the cooldown removal the class doesn't work.
  3. Skill lines on the staff or guard. Active skill lines belong on rings.
  4. Skipping Accuracy and chasing Crit past 50%. Dark Crush gets parried and the window is wasted.
  5. Holding Dark Crush instead of cancelling from Onslaught. You lose 9–12% damage.
