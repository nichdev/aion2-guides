Step-by-Step

1

The goal

Take a brand-new character to 1700+ GS in under 50 hours, with no alts and no P2W.

The character shown in the video is Lv45, 1705 GS, with 47.6 hours played.

2

Finish the story and level the Verteron Monolith

  • Finish all main story quests.
  • Level the Verteron Monolith to 30.
  • Clear every side quest, Sealed Dungeon and Stronghold.
3

Grab every feather and yellow question mark

Pick up every feather and every yellow question mark as you go. That way you don't have to backtrack when the Ascension Gauge Level 1 requirement shows up.

4

Don't enhance early story gear

Early story gear gets replaced quickly, so leave it alone.

⚠
Don't do this: enhancing early story gear. It gets replaced quickly and wastes materials.
5

Expeditions: save your energy

In Expeditions (for example Krao Cave), kill the boss but save all your energy for later. You'll spend it in Step 15.

⚠
Don't do this: spending energy on the chest after you kill the boss.
6

Revelation Amulet and Noble Belt: Substance Morph

The Revelation Amulet and Noble Belt are the only story items worth enhancing.

  • Gear tab > Substance Morph: +10 Green becomes Blue, and +10 Blue becomes Yellow.
  • Leaving the belt at Blue is fine.
  • Take the Amulet to Yellow as soon as you can.
7

Side missions and Item Extraction

Side missions give enhancing materials and money. Dismantle old gear with Item Extraction so your inventory doesn't fill up.

8

Skills and the Daevanion board

Use the skill materials from Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds to level skills and earn Daevanion points.

Node GS: Gray +1 Green +2 Blue +3 Orange +4

Aim for Orange tiles along a path through your class's useful skills.

9

Draupnir expedition: trigger pity for Bakarma Boots

  1. First clear: take the double reward.
  2. Second clear: take the single reward.

This triggers the pity guaranteed reward. Pick Bakarma Boots.

10

Enhance and socket your gear

  • Enhance every Blue piece to +3 and every Yellow piece to +10.
  • Add Lesser Manastones (accessories) and Soulstones (armor/weapons), aiming for at least one green stat per piece.

Stat GS: Gray +1 Green +2 Blue +3 Yellow +4

11

Weekly Ascension Trial and the Clash Rune

Milestone: ~1200+ GS
  • Run the weekly Ascension Trial (Sanctum of Loathing) for Manastones and Soulstones.
  • Run the highest difficulty your GS allows, right before the weekly reset.
  • Enhance the Clash Rune to +1 and stop there.
⚠
Don't do this: enhancing the Clash Rune past +1. It's destroyed if an enhancement fails.
12

The Abyss (PvP zone)

Main menu > The Abyss (PvP zone):

  • Collect feathers for the Ereshkigal Monolith.
  • Do the weekly command quest.
  • Points go to the PvP Daevanion board. Prioritize PvP Attack and Defense orange tiles.
  • Spend Abyss points in the Trade Shop on Stigma Shards.
13

Vakron Sky Island expedition

Milestone: 1400+ GS

Use the same claim pattern as Draupnir (double on the first clear, single on the second) to trigger pity. Then pick the Vakron piece that adds the most GS.

14

Timed events: Spacetime Rift and Shugo Festival

All times are Server Time. See the events schedule.

  • Spacetime Rift: one hour in enemy territory clearing Sealed Dungeons.
  • Shugo Festival: claim keys (free players get 2 a day, max 14) and spend the currency in the Festival Shop on Daevanion Crystals.
⚠
Don't do this: touching enemy teleport stones in the Spacetime Rift. They kill you instantly.
15

Urugugu Canyon: spend all your saved energy

Milestone: 1600 GS
  • Spend all your saved energy on Urugugu Canyon (conquest expedition) for accessories and yellow weapons.
  • Pity takes 21 rewards (10 double claims + 1 single claim). Pick an Autumnus Ring, Earrings or Necklace.
  • Get every Yellow piece to +10 with at least two green stats.
16

Transcendence Dungeons and Arcana cards

Milestone: 1700+ GS

At 1600 GS, clear stage 1 of both Transcendence Dungeons, Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis, for Arcana cards.

Arcana GS: Gray +20 Green +40

Just equip your best ones.

⚠
Don't do this: enhancing Arcana cards. Just equip your best ones.
