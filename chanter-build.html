⚡ Quick Start / TL;DR

  1. Dark Crush is your main damage skill. Its Piercing Strike follow-up chain is where most of your damage comes from. Push it toward Lv16, which unlocks the no-cooldown spec and is the biggest power spike for the class.
  2. Spinning Strike: take the -5s cooldown spec as early as you can.
  3. Stigmas you always run: Sprint Mantra and Undefeated Mantra. All three guides agree.
  4. Top passives: Wind's Promise and Attack Preparation. Add Impact Hit as well, since 2 of the 3 guides take it.
  5. Make one macro that fires a quick-slot column: Dark Crush (bottom) → Spinning Strike → Impactful Crush → Incandescent Blow / Marchutan's Wrath (top).
  6. Play it like this: hold Left Click (Onslaught) and your macro key (Right Click) at the same time.
  7. Early game: keep some sustain (Recuperation, Defiance). Endgame: you aren't a real healer, so focus on DPS.

01 How the Chanter does damage

You only need four ideas before you look at the skill tables.

🎯 The core

Dark Crush crits on hit and chains into Piercing Strike. All three guides build around this.

🔓 The spike

At Lv16, Dark Crush can take a spec that removes its cooldown. Antochi calls this crucial.

🪟 The windows

Spinning Strike and Impactful Crush open windows that let Dark Crush fire over and over.

📈 What to value

None of the videos gives a stat or gear priority. They do suggest that critical hit and cooldown reduction matter.

Reading the tables: Lv8 is the skill level a guide recommends. Lv11 max means "max it at this stage." 3 is a spec slot number. V1 V2 V3 show which video a recommendation comes from.

02 Your progression path

Most of the disagreements between the guides come from timing. Each guide is written for a different point in your Chanter's life.

1

Early game

~230–250 skill points. Follow V1: Lv8 core skills, a few maxed, plenty of sustain.

2

Early → mid game

Follow V3: reach Lv16 on Dark Crush and Onslaught, pick up Impact Hit, use the 2-step macro.

3

Endgame DPS

Follow V2: Lv20 core skills, 1-step macro, drop Marchutan's Wrath, pure DPS.

05 The macro: setup and column diagram

The macro presses a quick-slot column for you. In the diagrams below, Row 0 is at the bottom, the same way the column stacks in game, and Dark Crush always sits there.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Open Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro.
  2. Put your skills in a quick-slot column in the order shown in the column diagram (Row 0 at the bottom).
  3. Create a macro and set Runs to "only while key is held."
  4. Set Delay to 10 ms.
  5. Add one step that points to that quick-slot column (V2). If you use the V3 version, add a second step for Incandescent Blow.
  6. Bind the macro to Right Click. Onslaught stays on Left Click.
Why only one step? Antochi explains that every extra step makes each column fire less often. One step pointing to the column keeps Dark Crush firing as often as possible.

06 Rotation

Boss opener V3

  1. Cast Power of the Storm (party buffs).
  2. Use Rushing Smash to close the gap to the boss.
  3. Hold Left Click (Onslaught) and your macro key together.

The loop V2

  1. Hold Left Click (Onslaught) and Right Click (macro) at the same time.
  2. The macro fires Spinning Strike and Impactful Crush as they come up. They open windows for Dark Crush.
  3. During those windows, Dark Crush and its Piercing Strike chain fire again and again. That's where your damage comes from.
  4. Use Recuperation when the party needs healing, then go straight back to holding both buttons.
💡 Why holding both buttons works

Onslaught on Left Click is your constant filler. In the V3 build, its specs also take −1s off Spinning Strike's cooldown on every hit. The macro on Right Click runs only while held, at a 10 ms delay, so it keeps trying the column every time a skill comes off cooldown. Dark Crush sits at Row 0, so it always gets priority. Once you have the Lv16 no-cooldown spec, it fires repeatedly during each window.

✅ What all three guides agree on
  • Dark Crush + Piercing Strike is the core damage. The Lv16 no-cooldown spec is the big power spike.
  • Spinning Strike −5s cooldown, taken early.
  • Sprint Mantra + Undefeated Mantra are mandatory stigmas.

❓ What about stats and gear?

None of the three videos gives a stat or gear priority, so this guide doesn't make one up. The builds lean on crit specs (Dark Crush, Spinning Strike) and cooldown-reduction specs (Spinning Strike, Fracturing Blow, Power of the Storm, the no-cooldown Dark Crush), which suggests critical hit and cooldown reduction are valuable.
